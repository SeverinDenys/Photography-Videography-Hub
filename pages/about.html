Hi, I’m Denys 👋

Just like those who came before me, I’ve been shaped by this history. Living in Luzern, I carry a modern mirrorless camera — but behind every click is the same wonder Niépce had in 1826.

I’m on my journey to becoming a Front-End Developer, while exploring my love for landscape and nature photography. My hikes across Europe — from Iceland to Switzerland — continue the tradition of using technology to connect people with moments.

This website is my way of weaving together creativity, technology, and storytelling. A space to showcase my works, share resources, and inspire others who are just as passionate about capturing life.
